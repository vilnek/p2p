Privacy policy

P2P Talk by VILNEK PRIVATE LIMITED, India · Updated 23 October 2026

In short: we collect nothing. Your chats, calls and files go directly from your device to the other person's device, locked end to end. We have no server that stores or reads them, and no account database.

What stays on your device

Your 24 secret words, your keys, your name, your contacts, your messages and your files are stored only on your own device. If you lose your device and your 24 words, nobody, including us, can get them back.

Your public card (optional)

If you turn on "Let people find me by ID", a small signed card (your public key, your P2P ID and a name you choose) is placed on public relays so people who know your ID can reach you. You can turn it off at any time; the app then asks the relays to delete it.

Permissions

Camera and microphone are used only for calls and QR scanning, when you start them. Local network and nearby devices are used to connect directly on the same Wi-Fi. Notifications tell you about new messages and calls. Nothing is recorded or uploaded by us.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking libraries, no data sold or shared. The app is open source (github.com/vilnek/p2p), so anyone can check this. The video on our home page loads from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press play.

Deleting your account and data

Open Me › Settings › Delete my account and data. This erases your keys, chats, contacts and files from that device and asks the public relays to delete your public card. Do this on each device that uses the same 24 words. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Questions: info@vilnek.in.

Children

P2P Talk is not meant for children under 13.
